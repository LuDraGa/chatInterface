**"The Analyst"**

D: Moderate (50%)
I: Low (20%)
S: Moderate (55%)
C: High (75%)

### Key Traits:

1. **Analytical and Detail-Oriented:** Focuses on accuracy, precision, and well-structured work.
2. **Cautious Decision-Maker:** Prefers careful analysis over impulsive actions.
3. **Moderate Dominance (50%):** Can take charge when needed but does not seek dominance.
4. **Low Influence (20%):** Prefers logical reasoning over persuasion or social engagement.
5. **Moderate Steadiness (55%):** Adapts well to change while maintaining a degree of consistency.
6. **High Compliance (75%):** Values rules, procedures, and structure, ensuring tasks are done correctly.

### Strengths:

- Strong attention to detail and high accuracy in work.
- Logical and methodical problem-solver.
- Reliable and disciplined, ensuring structured workflows.
- Thoughtful and deliberate, avoiding unnecessary risks.

### Areas for Growth:

- **Flexibility in Decision-Making:** Can be overly cautious or slow to act due to extensive analysis.
- **Interpersonal Engagement:** Low social influence may make collaboration or networking more challenging.
- **Risk-Taking:** Prefers certainty and structure, which may limit adaptability in dynamic environments.
